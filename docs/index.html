AnyVoice turns any epub, pdf or txt into a full-cast audiobook — every character found and voiced automatically — and starts playing on your phone in seconds while the rest renders. On your own machine. Open source.

Three voices, two emotions, one scene tag — all decided by the pipeline, none by you.

Casts itself

An LLM reads each chapter, works out who says what, discovers characters as they appear, and tags emotion and setting. Voices are matched to gender and age and kept distinct.

Plays in seconds

Kokoro renders narration ~4× faster than realtime on a laptop. The pipeline stays a chapter ahead of wherever you are listening.

Recast anyone

Don't like a voice? Swap it. Only that character's lines re-render, starting from your position.

Sounds like a place

Rain, thunder, a hearth, a carriage, ballroom murmur — synthesised, license-free, mixed low under the voices. Off / low / mid.

Actually expressive

Optional Chatterbox pass re-renders dialogue with emotion in the background and swaps it in as it finishes.

Yours

Runs on your machine. Export a real .m4b with chapter markers. Your books never leave home except the text sent for casting.

How it works

Ingestepub / pdf / txt → clean chapters
Segmentnarration vs “quotes”, numbered
CastLLM labels speaker, emotion, scene — labels only, never the text
VoiceKokoro now, Chatterbox upgrade later
Playsegments stream as they land; ambience mixed in-browser

The LLM gets the chapter with quotes tagged [Q12] and returns only labels — about 30k output tokens for a whole novel, roughly $1 with Claude Opus 5, less with Kimi K3 or Sonnet. Voices cost nothing.

Quick start

git clone https://github.com/surajtripathy07/anyvoice-audiobooks && cd anyvoice-audiobooks
uv sync
mkdir -p models && cd models && \
  curl -LO https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/download/model-files-v1.0/kokoro-v1.0.onnx && \
  curl -LO https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/download/model-files-v1.0/voices-v1.0.bin && cd ..
cp .env.example .env     # add one LLM key
./run.sh                 # → http://localhost:8080  ·  phone: http://<your-ip>:8080
Needs
Python 3.10+ with uv, ffmpegany Mac / Linux; Windows should work, untested
~400 MB Kokoro modeltwo files, downloaded once
One LLM keyAnthropic, OpenRouter (Kimi K3…) or OpenAI. Optional — a regex fallback works, roughly
Chatterbox (optional)~3 GB, Apple Silicon or CUDA, for expressive dialogue

Honest status

A few days old. Tested end to end on Pride and Prejudice. PDF extraction is heuristic. Single-quote dialogue isn't split yet. Chatterbox trails realtime on a laptop, which is why it runs as a background upgrade. If you try it and something breaks, open an issue with the book's source and a short excerpt.

Copyright, plainly

AnyVoice converts books you have, for your own listening, on your own hardware — like a screen reader. It stores nothing outside your machine. Don't redistribute audio made from copyrighted books. Public-domain works are fair game for anything.
